What is inside

Three tools that small service businesses normally buy separately — unified into one workflow with shared contacts, deals and bookings.

⊜

CRM

Pipelines, deals, tasks, bookings, contacts. Server-side filters and saved presets. Custom fields per pipeline. Inbox unifying WhatsApp, Instagram and web forms.

₽

Finance

Income and expenses with immutable transactions. Corrections create new records, never overwrite — a real audit trail. Multi-currency, account-aware reporting.

▷

SMM scheduler

Plan and publish to Instagram, Telegram and LinkedIn from one calendar. Reuse the same media library across channels and track performance per account.

Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 8 May 2026

If you want us to delete personal data we hold about you, please follow the steps below. This instruction page is published per Meta Platform Terms requirement for apps that access user data.

Self-service deletion

Inside ProfBro you can delete individual contacts, messages, deals and files at any time. You can also disconnect a WhatsApp Business Account from Settings → Channels, which immediately stops further message ingestion and revokes our access tokens.

Full account deletion

  1. Send an email from your registered address to k.roletskiy@profbroker.com with the subject “Delete my ProfBro account”.
  2. Include the workspace slug (subdomain) and the email of the workspace owner.
  3. We will confirm receipt within 3 business days and complete deletion within 30 calendar days. Backups containing residual data are purged within an additional 30 days.

WhatsApp message data

WhatsApp messages received via the Embedded Signup integration are stored in your workspace. They are deleted together with the workspace per the process above. Disconnecting the channel alone retains historical messages in your inbox unless you also delete them manually.
